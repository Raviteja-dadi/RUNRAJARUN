Royal Expedition Guide

SACRED CHARIOTS & SURVIVAL

"Colossal sacred chariots are not just obstacles — they are core mechanics that dictate your movement, scoring strategy, and survival."

🪜 How to Board a Chariot
  • The Ramp Launcher: Rather than trying to jump directly onto a high chariot, aim for the slanted wooden ramp placed directly in front. Running straight onto this ramp automatically launches you smoothly onto the chariot roof.
  • Super Sneaker Boost: Finding Super Sneakers massively boosts your vertical jump height, letting you leap over shorter structures and easily clear wide gaps.
🛣️ Mechanics on Chariot Roofs
  • Absolute Ground Safety: Running on roofs keeps you safe from all lower ground hazards (ancient urns, stone spikes, pools).
  • High-Value Rewards: Roofs are prime spots for lines of gold coins, Mystery Boxes, and premium power-ups.
  • Linked Chariot Convoys: Frequently, 2 or 3 chariots link back-to-back. Run across the entire convoy to bypass ground obstacles!
  • Limited Mobility: Side-to-side movements are restricted. Always plan your exit if barriers are ahead!
🏃 How to Dismount a Chariot
  • Reaching the End: At the edge of a chariot roof, you smoothly drop back down to the ground.
  • Mid-Air Swipes: Swipe left or right at any time on a roof to jump off early to an adjacent track.
  • The Post-Roof Danger: Watch out for barriers immediately following the roof! Swipe down to roll instantly on landing.
💡 Advanced Formations
  • Staggered Snake Pattern: Interleaved chariots across adjacent tracks force constant side-to-side weaving, testing your instant lane instincts.
  • Hoverboard Insurance: Riding a Hoverboard absorbs head-on chariot collisions!
